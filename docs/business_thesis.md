# Antigravity: The $1B Institutional Liquidity Hub

## 1. The Multi-Trillion Dollar Problem
The global **Correspondent Banking** network is the backbone of international finance, yet it is fundamentally broken.
- **Fragmented Liquidity**: Banks maintain $27 Trillion in "Nostro/Vostro" accounts globally to settle cross-border trades.
- **Opportunity Cost**: This $27T earns **0% yield** and is locked in siloed ledgers (JPM, HSBC, Citi).
- **Settlement Lag**: Transacting takes T+2 or T+3 days due to manual reconciliations.

## 2. Our Solution: Antigravity Hub
Antigravity replaces the "50 accounts" model with a **Single Global Treasury** on Solana.
- **Unified Ledger**: Multi-asset institutional mints ($AG-USD, $AG-GOLD) settle in < 400ms.
- **Protocol-Level Compliance**: IVMS 101 Travel Rule rules are baked into the **Transfer Hooks**.
- **Automated Yield Sweep**: Idle cash is never stagnant; the **Yield Engine** routes excess capital to liquid yield vaults (e.g., Solstice Finance) via CPI.

## 3. The Million-Dollar Business Model
Antigravity generates revenue from the **Velocity of Money**:
1. **Performance Fee (10 bps)**: We take a 0.1% fee on every "Automated Sweep" transaction. If an institution sweeps $10B/year, that is **$10M in pure protocol revenue**.
2. **Gateway SaaS ($5,000/mo)**: Institutions pay for the Compliance and SDP gateways as Managed Infrastructure.
3. **Yield Spread**: We capture a 5% "Performance Fee" on the excess interest generated for the client.

## 4. Why Solana? (The Killer Advantage)
- **Deterministic Latency**: Institutional settlement requires sub-second finality ($400ms).
- **Token-2022 Extensions**: No other L1 offers **Transfer Hooks** and **Permanent Delegates** natively, allowing us to enforce KYC at the bytecode level.
- **High Throughput**: 100k+ TPS is required to support global inter-bank settlement volumes.
