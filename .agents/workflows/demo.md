3. Execute the 'Agentic Sweep':

- Go to the **Compliance Gate** tab.
- Click **RUN_SWARM_LOGIC**.

5. Generate the Final Proof:
- Return to the **Overview** tab.
- Click **GENERATE_AUDIT_REPORT**.
